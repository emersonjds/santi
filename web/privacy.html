Privacy

santi is an open-source tracker. This page says what this server keeps about you. If you run your own copy, you hold the same data on your own machines.

What is stored

  • Your account: email, display name and a password hash (argon2; the password itself is never stored).
  • Your work: projects, members, boards, sprints, issues, comments and labels you or your team create or import.
  • Sign-in records: a hash of each session and API token, so they can be revoked. Sessions end after 30 days or when you sign out.

What is not

  • No tracking, no analytics, no ads and no third-party scripts.
  • One cookie: the session cookie that keeps you signed in. It is HttpOnly and sent only over HTTPS.
  • Nothing is sold or shared with anyone.

Your choices

Revoke API tokens from your account page at any time. To delete your account or export your data, open an issue on GitHub and the operator of this server will handle it.
