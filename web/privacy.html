Privacy Policy

Version 2026-10-16 · Last updated: 16 October 2026

Your data is yours. santi exists to track your team's work, not to learn about you. This page says, in plain words, what this server keeps, why, for how long, who else touches it, and how to use your rights.

This text is written in plain language and is not legal advice; review by counsel is recommended.

The short version

  • We never sell or rent your personal data, and we share it only with the providers that run the service (listed below).
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics and no third-party scripts. Pages load only files from this server.
  • Cookies and browser storage are only the ones the app needs to work (listed below).
  • You can download your data or delete your account at any time, from the account page.

AI assistants (MCP and API tokens)

santi never sends your data to an AI provider by itself. If you connect an AI assistant to santi, through santi mcp or an API token, whatever the assistant reads is sent to the AI provider you chose, under that provider's terms and privacy policy. Revoke the token to stop it.

Your rights and how to use them

Under Brazil's LGPD (Lei 13.709/2018, art. 18) and, where it applies, the EU GDPR (arts. 15 to 22), you can:

  • Confirm and access what we hold: Account → Your data → Export my data downloads it as JSON.
  • Correct it: edit what you wrote in santi, or write to us about your account details.
  • Delete it: Account → Your data → Delete my account, or write to us.
  • Take it elsewhere (portability): the export is a plain JSON file any tool can read.
  • Know who receives it: the section above lists everyone.
  • Anonymisation, blocking or deletion of data that is unnecessary or handled against the law.
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interest, and withdraw consent where we rely on it.
  • Complain to the Brazilian data protection authority (ANPD, gov.br/anpd) or, in the EU, to your local supervisory authority.

Write to %VITE_PRIVACY_EMAIL% from the email on your account. It is free, and we answer within 15 days (LGPD art. 19). If we cannot confirm the request is yours, we will ask for something only the account holder knows.

What happens when you delete your account

  • Your name, email and password hash are erased at once; you are signed out everywhere and your API tokens stop working.
  • Projects only you belong to are deleted, with their issues and comments.
  • Where you are the last admin of a shared project, the member with the oldest account becomes admin.
  • Issues and comments you wrote in shared projects stay for your teammates, shown as "Deleted user".
  • The acceptance record stays, as described in the table above.

How we protect it

  • HTTPS everywhere; the session cookie is never sent over plain HTTP.
  • Passwords hashed with Argon2id; sessions and API tokens stored only as SHA-256 hashes.
  • A strict Content Security Policy, protection against cross-site requests, and rate limits on sign-in, sign-up and other sensitive actions.
  • The database is not reachable from the internet, and the code is open source, so anyone can check these claims.

Age

santi is for people 18 or older, and sign-up asks you to confirm it. If you believe a minor created an account, write to us and we will delete it.

Hosting santi yourself

santi is free software you can run on your own server. Then your data stays on your machines, you are its controller, and this policy covers only this hosted instance.

Changes

When this policy changes in a way that matters, the app shows the new version and asks you to accept it before you continue; the version and date at the top always identify the current text.
