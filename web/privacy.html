Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Your data is yours. santi exists to track your team's work, not to learn about you. This page says, in plain words, what this server keeps, why, and what you can do about it.

The short version

  • We never sell, rent or share your personal data.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics and no third-party scripts. Pages load only files from this server.
  • One cookie: the session cookie that keeps you signed in.
  • You can download your data or delete your account at any time.

Who is responsible

This instance of santi is run by %VITE_OPERATOR_NAME%, the controller of the personal data it holds (the controlador under the LGPD). Write to %VITE_PRIVACY_EMAIL% for any privacy request.

What we store and why

  • Your account: your name, your email and a hash of your password. We need them to sign you in and to show teammates who did what. Your password itself is never stored: only an Argon2id hash that cannot be turned back into it.
  • Your work: projects, boards, sprints, issues, comments, labels and the files you import (we keep the issues they contain, not the files). This is the service itself.
  • Sign-in records: a hash of each session and API token, so they can be checked and revoked. Sessions end after 30 days or when you sign out.
  • Terms acceptance: when you accepted the Terms of Use and which version, so we can show it.
  • Short-lived counters: to stop password guessing and abuse, the server counts recent attempts per network address and per email in memory. They are never written to disk and are forgotten within an hour or at the next restart.

We do not collect anything else: no device fingerprints, no location, no browsing history, no marketing profile.

Cookies

santi sets one cookie, __Host-santi_session, after you sign in. It is HttpOnly (scripts cannot read it), Secure (sent only over HTTPS) and SameSite=Lax. There are no advertising, analytics or third-party cookies, so there is no cookie banner to click through.

Legal basis

We process your data to provide the service you signed up for (performance of a contract: LGPD art. 7, V; GDPR art. 6(1)(b)) and, for security measures such as rate limiting and sign-in records, on our legitimate interest in keeping the service safe (LGPD art. 7, IX; GDPR art. 6(1)(f)). We do not rely on consent for anything else, and we do not use your data for advertising.

Where your data lives

On the server that hosts this instance: currently Railway, region %VITE_HOSTING_REGION%, in a PostgreSQL database that is not reachable from the internet. If that region is outside Brazil or the European Economic Area, the transfer relies on the hosting provider's contractual safeguards. Backups stay with the same provider.

How long we keep it

As long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your name, email and password hash are erased at once; projects nobody else uses are deleted with it. Backups roll over and disappear within 30 days.

Your rights

Under Brazil's LGPD (Lei 13.709/2018, art. 18) and, where it applies, the EU GDPR (arts. 15 to 22), you can:

  • Confirm and access what we hold: Account → Your data → Export my data downloads it as JSON.
  • Correct it: change what you wrote in santi, or write to us for your account details.
  • Delete it: Account → Your data → Delete my account, or write to us.
  • Take it elsewhere (portability): the export is a plain JSON file any tool can read.
  • Know who we share it with: nobody, other than the hosting provider that runs the server for us.
  • Withdraw consent and object: you can stop using santi and delete your account at any time.
  • Complain to the Brazilian data protection authority (ANPD) or, in the EU, to your local supervisory authority.

We answer requests sent to %VITE_PRIVACY_EMAIL% within 15 days. We may ask you to write from the email on your account, so we know the request is yours.

What happens when you delete your account

  • Your name, email and password hash are erased; you are signed out everywhere and your API tokens stop working.
  • Projects that only you belong to are deleted, with their issues and comments.
  • Where you are the last admin of a shared project, the member with the oldest account becomes admin.
  • Issues and comments you wrote in shared projects stay for your teammates, shown as "Deleted user".

How we protect it

  • HTTPS everywhere; the session cookie is never sent over plain HTTP.
  • Passwords hashed with Argon2id; sessions and API tokens stored only as SHA-256 hashes.
  • A strict Content Security Policy, protection against cross-site requests, and rate limits on sign-in, sign-up and other sensitive actions.
  • The code is open source, so anyone can check these claims.

Hosting santi yourself

santi is free software you can run on your own server. Then your data stays on your machines, you are its controller, and this policy covers only this hosted instance.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will say so in the app before it takes effect. The date at the top always shows the current version.
