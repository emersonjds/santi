# Pricing and sustainability

santi is free and open source, and every feature ships in the open code. What costs money is
running it for someone else.

| | Who it is for | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Self-host | teams with a server of their own | free, complete, no user or project limits; you pay only your own infrastructure |
| Hosted, Free | anyone: solo founders, startups, small teams, students, universities | free: unlimited people and projects, every feature, 2 GB of attachments per workspace (25 MB per file) |
| Hosted, Team | teams that outgrow the free storage or want help | US$ 19 a month flat per workspace (US$ 190 a year): 100 GB of attachments, e-mail support, downloadable backups |
| Enterprise | large companies, hosted or self-hosted | single sign-on (SAML), SCIM, audit log, SLA, data residency, white-label and dedicated support, licensed on top of the same code or as a contract |
| Services | teams moving from another tracker | migration and support, quoted per case |

The hosted plans are not open yet; self-hosting works today ([Self-hosting](self-hosting.md)). The
public instance that runs santi's own backlog costs about US$ 20 a month (app, PostgreSQL and the
attachments bucket). Hosting, enterprise add-ons and services fund the work, never features locked out of the code: every plan,
and self-host, has the same boards, backlog, sprints, reports, CLI and MCP.
