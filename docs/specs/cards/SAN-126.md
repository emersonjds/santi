# SAN-126 / SAN-127 — Short person names in project tables

Owner, 2026-10-17: the Projects list Lead column truncates a long all-caps name ("EMERSON JUNIOR D…"), and the Trash "Trashed" cell wraps "21 hours ago by EMERSON JUNIOR DANIEL SILVA" over three lines. No panel ran: SAN-100 already decided how a person's name is shortened (`shortName`, `fullName` in `web/src/shared/lib/initials.ts`), and the issue card menu uses it.

## Decisions

| # | Question | Winner |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Name format in a table cell | `shortName`: first and last word ("Emerson Silva"), all-caps names in title case (SAN-100). The stored name never changes. |
| 2 | Full name | `title` with `fullName` on the cell text, the pattern of the card menu. |
| 3 | Lead cell | `UserAvatar` (as on the Members page) followed by the short name, one line, truncated only as a last resort. |
| 4 | Archived/Trashed cell | "21 hours ago by Emerson Silva": relative time and short name; full name in `title`. |

## Acceptance

- Lead shows avatar + "Emerson Silva" for "EMERSON JUNIOR DANIEL SILVA"; hovering shows the full name in title case.
- Trashed and Archived cells read "<time> by <short name>" and fit on one line at 1440, at most two at 375.
- One- and two-word names show unchanged; an empty lead or actor shows what it shows today.
- States: 1440 and 375, light and dark, no horizontal scroll at 375.

## Out of scope

- Other tables or lists that show people (backlog assignee, board filters already use the helpers).
