# SAN-221 — Hosted Team plan: pricing on the landing (slice 1)

The plan exists in `docs/pricing.md` but not on the landing; the first interested company asks how to pay. Slice 1 is the landing section only. Stripe checkout, webhook, Customer Portal and Free-plan limits are later slices of this card.

## Context

- Business model (decided 2026-10-20, not reopened here): self-host free and complete, every feature in the open code; hosting and services fund the work, never features locked out of the code. AGPL-3.0 + trademark policy (D61).
- Price (market check 2026-10-22): Team US$ 19/month flat per workspace, annual US$ 190 (two months off), R$ 97 / R$ 970 in Brazil. Unlimited projects; fair use up to 50 people per workspace (a notice above it, no block). Free hosted: 5 people, 1 project, 1 GB of attachments. Round number on purpose, no .99. Cheaper than per-seat rivals from 3 people up (Jira Standard ~US$ 43 for 5 people, Linear ~US$ 60).
- Hosted plans are not open yet: no billing exists; anyone can sign up on getsanti.app today and nothing is limited yet.
- Owner, 2026-10-22: **emphasize that santi is open source for self-hosted use.**
- Landing (`web/landing.html`, static HTML, no React; `app/styles/landing.css`; D39 budgets: JS ≤ 10 KB, CSS ≤ 10 KB gzip; PageSpeed 100, SAN-111). Sections today: hero → features → perf band → `#self-host` → "Coming next" band (stale: Reports and Notifications already shipped) → `#your-data` → `#faq` → CTA → footer. Nav: Features, Self-host, FAQ, GitHub, Sign in, Try santi. Card grid pattern: `<ul class="grid … md:grid-cols-3">` with `li.card`, `.badge`, `.card-title`, `.card-text`, `ul.checks`.
- The FAQ "Is santi free?" answers only about open source.
